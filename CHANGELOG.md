# Crafter's Journal

## 0.67.0

- **Your Investments, scans and prices are back after the Forever client update.** Since the game client update of November 21, the game no longer tells addons which realm you're on, so Investments, your AH scans, prices and your alts' known recipes all showed up empty, even though they were still saved. In Forever the auction house is shared by your ruleset (Normal, PvP, Roleplay...) and faction, so the addon now keeps everything by ruleset and faction, which the game still reports. The first time you log in, what you had saved moves over to the new keys, including anything you added after the update. Each old realm goes to the ruleset of the characters who played there. Note: an older version of the addon won't see the moved data.
- **Closing the window in combat no longer gets blocked.** Once a route had shown a "Learn recipe" row (a recipe scroll in your bags), the window couldn't be closed with Escape, hidden or moved during combat: the game showed "Crafter's Journal has been blocked from an action". The button that uses the scroll now sits on top of the row only while your mouse is over it, like the star on trainers and vendors, and steps aside when combat starts. Clicking the row out of combat still learns the recipe; in combat it does nothing. (GitHub #11)
- **Listing on the AH works when the Auction House asks you to confirm.** Sometimes the Auction House asks you to confirm a listing (usually for an unusual price). For listings made from Crafter's Journal, the game's confirmation window did nothing when you clicked Accept, and a few seconds later the chat said the Auction House didn't confirm it. The addon now asks itself, with what you're listing and at what price: Yes lists it, No (or Escape, or closing the AH) cancels it. Listings made from the game's own auction screen are unchanged. (GitHub #10)
- **Routes no longer use specialization recipes you can't learn.** Patterns, plans and schematics that need a specialization (Tribal, Elemental or Dragonscale Leatherworking, Armorsmith, Weaponsmith and the Master Sword/Hammer/Axesmith, Gnomish or Goblin Engineering) were offered as if anyone could learn them: the Leatherworking route used Frostsaber Boots (Tribal) at 251-280 and the Blacksmithing route Blazing Rapier (Master Swordsmith) at 266-289. Now the route and its alternatives only use them if you have that specialization, Make Money no longer suggests learning them (this also applied to trainer recipes such as Goblin Rocket Helmet), and the recipe collection says which specialization they need and no longer lists them as "you can learn it now". Learning a specialization recalculates the route and Make Money.
- **Routes use the recipes you know from your other professions.** A miner leveling Blacksmithing was told to buy Mithril Bars with 76 Mithril Ore in their bags. Now, if your character knows the recipe, the route smelts the ore (or makes the Heavy Leather, the bolts of cloth...) instead of buying, when that's cheaper, and the shopping list asks for the ore. With "Use what's in your bags", the ore you carry counts too. The row says which profession it's from ("Craft 40× Mithril Bar (with Mining)"), its click opens that recipe, and its ✕ makes the route buy the bars again. Only recipes you already know: the route never suggests learning another profession's recipe, and transmutes and other cooldown recipes aren't used when the AH sells the material. Learning one of these recipes recalculates the routes that use it. (CurseForge)
- **The route explains its numbers.** Each step's row now shows the net per craft (materials minus what you get back), the one that matches the amount on the right, instead of only the materials. Its tooltip ends with the step's numbers: materials, what you get back selling it (or keeping it for later steps) and the net. In "Why this one and not another", each alternative says how much more it costs in total and why: its net per craft and how many crafts it needs, next to the route's. A line under the summary says how the route is chosen (the lowest net cost: materials minus vendor sales, which is why it may not match guides), and the Total's tooltip shows its sum. When buying by stages asks for more than the average (312 bars when 288 are needed on average), the tooltip says it's a margin in case points come slower, and that what's left is used in the next stage. (CurseForge)
- **Routes skip recipes on cooldown.** The Tailoring route asked you to make Mooncloth while it was on cooldown. Now a recipe you know that is on cooldown (the addon sees it when you open the profession) is left out of the route until it's ready, and then the route uses it again. (Discord)
- **Expert Cooking and Expert First Aid don't need level 35.** The route said the Expert book needs level 35, but in Forever it has no level requirement. Artisan still needs 35, for its quest.

## 0.66.0

- **The Calculator prices AH materials like the routes do.** It multiplied the cheapest auction by the quantity, so with large quantities the material cost came out below what you'd really pay. It now climbs the AH price ladder (the cheapest units first, then the next ones), and if you need more than what's for sale, the rest counts at the median price. The price per unit shown is the average you'd pay.

- **New tab: Investments.** Keep track of what you buy to resell. Add each purchase by hand (quantity and price, per unit or as a total) by typing the item's name in its search box (it only offers items that can go to the AH). Each item shows how many you have, your average price, the price now and an arrow with how much it's up or down since your average. Sales use the average-cost method: selling doesn't change the average of what's left, and what you sold counts as realized profit. Values and profits are net of the AH's 5% cut. Click an item for its detail: price now, your average, change since your average and over the last 4 days, a chart of the last days and its purchases and sales, which you can delete. Prices and the chart come from Auctionator's price history (needs Auctionator); days that drop and bounce back (one very cheap auction) are greyed out and don't count, and prices older than 3 days are shown in grey. The portfolio is for your whole account: all your characters on the same realm and faction share it. The price you type is the total by default; in /cj options, Investments, you can make it the price per unit, and the purchase window lets you switch it each time. Farmed something? Note it down at price 0: it shows "Farmed" instead of a percentage, and the "If sold" column counts it all as profit.
- **Scanning goes through Auctionator.** The Scan button (in the header and above the AH) and /cj scan start Auctionator's full scan, and the addon reads those same auctions for every tab: one scan for both, so they no longer compete for the one full scan every 15 minutes the server allows. Crafter's Journal no longer requests a scan of its own. Without Auctionator the Scan button is greyed out and its tooltip says to install it; everything else keeps working with what the addon already had. Scans requested by other addons (Auctionator's automatic scan when opening the AH, ForeverForge...) are always read, so the "Read other addons' scans" option is gone.
- **No more "action blocked" warning when using items from your bags.** Opening a box from your bags could show the game's "Crafter's Journal has been blocked from an action" window, mostly alongside other addons such as EllesmereUI, even though Crafter's Journal wasn't doing anything with that item. The addon no longer sits in front of the game's Lua error handler. Blocked actions are still logged in /cj errors; to log the addon's Lua errors too, type /cj errors record (it lasts until the next reload).
- **Selling part of a stack works with full bags.** To sell only part of a stack at a vendor, the addon splits it into a free slot; if your normal bags were full it gave up, even with room in the reagent bag. It now uses the reagent bag too (if it doesn't accept the item, the stack goes back where it was).

## 0.63.0

- **Your reagent bag counts.** What you carry in Forever's reagent bag now counts in the route, the Inventory and Make Money. "Collect your mail" didn't complete when what you collected (herbs, for example) went into that bag.
- **Recipes with two tools.** Engineering helmets need both the Blacksmith Hammer and the Arclight Spanner, but only the first tool of each recipe was read, so the route never asked for the spanner (134 recipes).
- **Use up what's left over.** When a step is done and you have materials left over, the route suggests crafting with them, but only when that earns more than selling them.
- **Recipes for intermediates.** If an intermediate needs a recipe you don't know (Stonescale Oil), the route now has a row to learn it, and its price counts in the route total.
- **Materials bought on the AH and from a vendor.** When part of a material is bought on the AH and the rest from a vendor, the route now lists it where most of it is bought (all the Crystal Vials were listed at the AH because 4 of them were cheaper there).
- **Steps already started count from your level**, even while the recipe is still to be learned.
- **Exact material amounts.** Each step's materials now match its crafts rounded up, instead of the fractional average (thanks to Saritus).
- **More reliable posting confirmation.** Yes posts before closing the prompt, the prompt closes when you close the AH or the vendor, and it no longer says "Posted" when the AH doesn't answer.

## 0.61.0

- **New: route assistant (beta).** With a route started, a small banner tells you what's next: craft, buy on the AH or from a vendor, collect your mail or train. One click does it or takes you there. Open it with the button next to Leave route or with /cj mini. Above it, an arrow shows the yards and time to the trainer, vendor or mailbox, and the destination is marked on the map with Shortest Path, TomTom or the game's waypoint. Skip marks a step as done when you got it elsewhere (trade chat, guild, another character), and you can also skip learning the profession to go to the AH first. Its ? answers the most common questions. It's in beta: if you see something odd, open a post in the bugs channel on our Discord.
- **Routes prefer safer recipes over long green stretches.** When choosing recipes, green skill-ups are now counted with a bit of bad luck on top of the average. Between an expensive green and an orange or yellow recipe that costs about the same, the route now picks the safe one. In our tests, routes use about half as many green points and a fifth fewer crafts, for 1-2% more gold. The cost the route shows is still the average.
- **Fewer trips to buy on green recipes.** Buying by stage on green recipes bought exactly the expected amount, so half the time it ran short and you had to go back again and again. It now buys with some margin. The batch size can be changed in /cj options, Shopping list (20 to 150 crafts, 50 by default).
- **Buying the whole route works like one big batch.** With the shopping list set to the whole route, each craft used up materials and the route sent you to buy the one that was now missing (1 Bolt of Linen Cloth). Once you've bought it all, it now asks for nothing while you can keep crafting, just like buying by stage. And at the AH, the recipes the route buys there later come right after the materials, in the same stop, so you don't go back for each one.
- **"This batch: N".** When what you carry covers fewer crafts than the step needs, the step says how many it covers. With luck it's enough; if not, the route sends you to buy more when you run out.
- **"Craft N×" no longer subtracts your bags twice.** With "use what's in your bags" on, an intermediate you already carried (Bolt of Silk Cloth) was subtracted twice, so the row asked you to craft too few. It now shows the real total and subtracts your bags once.
- **Profit with recipes that make several items.** Weapon oils, Fire Oil and other recipes that make 2 per craft were shown with the cost of a whole craft next to the price of a single item, so they looked like a loss. Make Money now shows real per-item cost and profit, row titles say how many items you'll get ("5× Shadow Oil (10 units)"), and the Collection and tooltips show the price of the whole craft.
- **No reliable AH price? To the vendor.** Items with no AH price, or one too risky to list (it may not sell and you'd lose the deposit), now go to Sell to vendor with a note on the row, instead of a separate "your call" section that offered them to nobody. Ctrl+right-click a row to move an item between the AH and the vendor.
- **Sell all sells everything in one click.** Selling many items at once (17 belts that don't stack) made the server skip some, and you had to click again. Items are now sold one after another.
- **"There wasn't enough" only when it's true.** It showed up on a new route, or after opening the AH just to scan. It now only appears after you actually tried to buy that material on this route.
- **Mail before the vendor, and the closest first.** The route now sends you to collect your AH purchases right after the AH, and if both the mailbox and a vendor are pending, to whichever is closer.
- **An intermediate you can't make no longer blocks the route.** With no materials for even one (1 Linen Cloth for Bolt of Linen Cloth) but enough to keep crafting the step, the route lets you continue and buys them with the next batch.
- **How long crafting takes.** The stats bar of a route has a new "Crafting time" column with the time to craft the whole route (without travel), and long or slow steps show their time with an hourglass. Intermediate rows ("Craft 136× Bolt of Linen Cloth") show it too.
- **What you'd get selling on the AH.** Under the route total, "with AH" shows what you'd end up with if you sell what you craft as the Profit view suggests, on the AH and to the vendor. The total above it stays the safe one, everything sold to the vendor.
- **The chat says what you're crafting** ("Crafting 26× Linen Belt..."), so crafting the wrong recipe by mistake shows right away.
- **At your rank cap, training is the only current step.** A crafting step that crossed the rank's level could stay current next to "Train Expert".
- **The Auction House button always opens the addon.** While the 15-minute server limit between full scans was counting down, the "Crafter's Journal" button above the Auction House was greyed out, so there was no way to open the addon from there. It can now always be clicked: it opens the window and scans only if a scan is allowed; otherwise you see your last prices, and the tooltip says when you can scan again.
- **Tidier route rows.** Each step no longer adds "2nd: …" with the next-best recipe to its row. It's still in the step's tooltip, with its numbers and why the route doesn't use it. Rows to get a recipe no longer say "X to remove it": the X is right there on the row.
- **A proper "about" sign.** Approximate numbers in the route ("~104 more crafts") now use a small ≈ icon instead of "~", which looked like a typo.

## 0.59.0

- **Confirm before selling or buying everything.** Sell all and Buy all in the vendor window, and the sell-to-vendor button in the Inventory tab, now ask first, saying what they'll sell or buy and for how much. Each prompt has a "Don't show again" checkbox and can be turned back on in the options, separately for each window.
- **Remove recipes from Make Money.** Each row has an X to stop suggesting that recipe (Discord: it suggested cooldown recipes and cooking recipes that need an expensive station). A row at the end of the list brings back the ones you removed.
- **"Craft N×" says how many are left.** Next to a step that makes the same item, "Craft 149×" was unclear. The row now says how many you still have to craft, and below it how many the route needs in total and how many you already carry.
- **First step: learn the profession.** If you haven't learned a profession yet, its route now starts with a row to learn it at the nearest trainer. With the trainer open, clicking it learns it right there; if you already have two primary professions, it tells you to unlearn one first.
- **Opening a trainer no longer empties your route.** Forever's trainer lists Linen Bag at "Tailoring 5, 1 silver" even though you get it for free with the profession. Opening the Tailoring trainer took that as the truth, and a started 1-150 route that used Linen Bag from level 1 lost all its steps. Recipes that come with the profession now stay free from level 1. And if a trainer ever really makes a started route impossible, the addon now drops it and calculates a new one from your level instead of leaving it empty.
- **Trainer prices are read in full again.** Every time you opened a trainer, reading its full list failed on Forever ("Missing on/off parameter"), so only what the trainer's filters showed was read.
- **Warning about huge numbers of crafts.** When a route crafts an intermediate in absurd amounts (usually because none was for sale in your last scan, so it makes the whole chain from scratch), the row and the status line say so, and suggest scanning again, buying it ready-made or turning on "Fewer crafts".
- **What's New can be turned off.** The What's New window has a "Don't show again" checkbox, and an option under General.
- **Mail is no longer read.** Tracking AH purchases waiting in your mailbox could leave items "in the mail" forever and make the shopping list ask for less than you needed. The addon no longer reads or saves anything from your mail.
- **Make Money uses TSM's demand data.** If you have TSM with its desktop app, its sales data for your region becomes one more market sign: how many sell per day and what share of listings actually sell. Low demand on its own marks a recipe "watch"; together with another warning sign, "avoid". Make Money also never suggests crafting more than what sells across the region in 2 days. The market tooltip shows a "Demand (TSM, region)" line and warns when TSM's average sale price is well below the price used here. TSM data only adds caution: your own scans still decide. "Recipes you could learn" no longer suggests paying for a recipe that TSM says barely sells, or that sells well below the price used here. Both limits can be adjusted with two sliders in the Make Money options: minimum sold per day (1 by default) and minimum sale rate (0.15 by default); setting one to 0 turns it off. Without TSM nothing changes, and it can be turned off in the Make Money options.
- **Cooldown recipes in today's batch.** A profitable transmute or Mooncloth whose cooldown is ready now goes into today's batch as a single craft, at the top, with its materials on the shopping list and a click to craft it. The addon reads the cooldown when you open the profession or craft it, and remembers it per character. While it's on cooldown it stays out of the batch and comes back by itself when it's ready. If the addon hasn't seen the cooldown yet, the row says so, and clicking it opens the profession to check. The Cooldowns section now uses the same cooldown detection as routes (Transmute: Elemental Fire has no cooldown on Forever) and its rows can be clicked to craft.
- **Cooldown recipes you could learn.** "Recipes you could learn" never suggested a cooldown recipe, even a very profitable one like Mooncloth. Now they're listed, counting one craft at a time: the row says it has a cooldown, and the tooltip says in how many crafts the recipe pays for itself.
- **Routes send you to vendors that really sell each material.** Who sells each crafting material came from Classic data, where almost every trade supplier sells Rune Thread; on Forever many don't, so the addon could send you to the wrong vendor. It now uses Forever's own vendor lists for 107 crafting materials, with each vendor's Forever location.
- **Materials you've already converted count.** When a route turns a material into another (Runecloth into Bolt of Runecloth), the Materials view kept asking you to buy the Runecloth you had just used up. What you carry already converted now counts as the material it came from, in the Materials view, the shopping list and the route's status line.
- **"Craft N×" rows follow your skill.** In a started route, the row for an intermediate (Craft 18× Bolt of Runecloth) always counted what the whole route needed from its start, even if you'd already done most of the step. It now counts only what the levels you have left still need, and disappears when nothing is left to make.
- **The Calculator no longer breaks with many materials.** A recipe with lots of materials pushed the profit, the "Sell on" selector and the buttons out of the panel. The materials now scroll in their own area (mouse wheel, with a guide on the right) when they don't fit; when they do, the sheet looks the same as before.
- **No more blocked-action error when opening a route in combat.** Opening a profession's route during combat made the game block one action per row (it lets clicks on an item icon reach its row). That setting now waits until combat ends.

## 0.58.0

- **Options in their own window.** A gear next to the language selector opens the addon's options, sorted by category on the left: General and one per tab or window (Shopping list, Auction House, Vendor, Inventory, Make Money). It also has a link to our Discord. "All options" in the gear menu of the windows next to the vendor, the AH and your profession opens it right where that window's options are. The options are no longer in the game's AddOns panel.
- **The vendor is only suggested what isn't worth more on the AH.** Inventory and the vendor window used to suggest selling to the vendor things that sell for more on the AH. Now they only suggest what the AH doesn't want (gray items, or things worth more at the vendor). Items with no reliable AH price are listed apart and can still be sold with a click, but aren't sold automatically.
- **Auctionator or TSM prices as a backup.** If your scan doesn't have an item from your bags, the price stored by Auctionator or TSM is used to decide whether it goes to the AH or the vendor. Your own scan always comes first, and it can be turned off in the options.
- **List everything despite the alerts.** When market alerts leave only a few units to list in the AH sell window ("5 of 77"), Ctrl-click lists all of them, warning how much deposit you risk if they don't sell.
- **Routes explain their second-best recipe.** When another recipe is cheaper in gold but the route doesn't use it, the step says why: learning it for a few crafts isn't worth it, it adds more crafts or an extra step, or the route couldn't be followed in order.
- **At your rank's cap, the route sends you to train.** When you reach the top of your rank (150 without Expert, for example) without training the next one, the route's current step is now training it, instead of a crafting step that no longer gives points.
- **"Vendor sales" instead of "Profit".** The route's summary strip called vendor income "Profit". It's now "Vendor sales", and its tooltip explains what it counts and that the Profit view shows what's worth listing on the AH.
- **Forever hotfixes in the data.** Red Mageweave Bag, Green and Black Silk Pack, and Green and Red Woolen Bag use the new amounts of cloth; Coarse Grinding Stone gives points up to 90 and the Tanning Rack goes gray 15 points earlier; Minor Arcane Elixir and Iron Grenade use their new vendor prices.
- **Make Money's status line is no longer cut off.** When it's long, it now wraps onto a second line and the list moves down to make room.

## 0.57.0

- **Vendor prices always come from the game.** The addon used to "learn" what vendors pay from the money you received after selling. When the game paid two sales at once, it could credit everything to one item and learn twice its price (Barbaric Leggings at 63s instead of 31s 51c), so Make Money promised twice the profit. Learning from sales is gone: vendor prices are the game's own, which match what vendors pay, and any price learned by older versions is cleared when you log in.
- **Routes only send you to learn a recipe for a few crafts when it's worth it.** A route could ask you to learn a new recipe for just 1-5 crafts because it saved a couple of silver, which isn't worth the trip to the trainer. Now, for a short step like that, the route tries those levels with the recipe next to it, and keeps the new recipe only if it saves more than 5s.
- **You can see at a glance when to wait before posting.** While the AH is being scanned, the AH sell window is covered by a dark layer with an hourglass inside a spinning ring and the scan's progress, and its rows can't be clicked. When the scan ends, the layer goes away and the prices are the current ones. Before, only a small orange line at the top said so.

## 0.56.0

- **Routes show how long crafting takes.** Some Forever recipes take 30-50 seconds per craft. A route step now shows its crafting time when each craft is slow (in orange) or the step is long, and its tooltip gives the time per craft and for the whole step. The route's Total row says how long the whole route takes to craft. If a step is too slow for you, remove it with its X and the route picks another recipe.
- **"Fewer crafts" also avoids slow recipes.** With the pocket-watch button on, each craft now weighs as much as it takes: a 50-second craft counts like 14 normal ones, so the route prefers faster recipes even if they cost a bit more. With the button off, the route stays the cheapest, as before.
- **New Merchant's Favor tab.** The cheapest way to earn Merchant's Favor with Waylaid Crates, using your AH scan:
  - **Your crates:** for each crate in your bags, the cheapest bundle to fill it with, and whether you already have it.
  - **Crates on the AH:** every crate sorted by what each point of Favor costs (crate + bundle). Bundles you can craft with recipes you know (Bolt of Linen Cloth, Cured Light Hide...) are priced both ways.
  - **Shopping list:** click a row to add the crate and its bundle.
  - The top of the tab shows your Merchant's Favor. The crates and their bundles come from the game itself. Artisan crates' Favor (~20 / ~40) is an estimate, because no guide lists it yet.
- **Remove a shopping list right from the AH.** In the small shopping list next to the Auction House, each list's title now has an ✕ that removes it, so old calculations don't pile up. Before, you could only remove lists from the Shopping list tab. A started route's list is only hidden and your route keeps going, as in the tab.
- **Performance: no more freezes, and less CPU out in the world.**
  - **No freeze after a scan or after login.** The market history behind "good / watch / avoid" (a week of scans) took about 0.35 s to read, all at once, the first time it was needed and again after every scan. It's now prepared in the background over a couple of seconds, without stopping the game. Windows keep using the previous one meanwhile and refresh once when it's ready.
  - **Crafting with the profession window open is smooth.** Each craft changes your bags, and Make Money recalculated every recipe's cost (about 20 ms per craft). Recipe costs are now kept until the next scan, and a bag change only redoes the light part (about 2 ms).
  - **Less CPU while questing.** Every weapon or defense skill-up (constant in combat) made the addon re-read all your professions and recheck your started routes. Now it only does that when a profession's skill or cap actually changes.
  - **Closed windows stay asleep.** The AH sell window, the window next to your profession and the addon's own pages no longer wake up on every looted item or item lookup from other addons while they're closed.
  - **Other addons' errors.** Lua errors from other addons are no longer inspected again each time they repeat. An addon throwing the same error every frame made Crafter's Journal look busy.
- **Make Money only crafts intermediates with recipes you know.** For Heavy Dynamite, its shopping list asked for Heavy Stone to make the Heavy Blasting Powder yourself, even if you didn't know that recipe. Make Money now buys an intermediate ready-made unless you can craft it. When you can and it's cheaper, the row's tooltip now says to craft it first (for example, "Craft these first: 684× Heavy Blasting Powder").
- **The gear on the windows next to the vendor, the AH and your profession opens a small menu.** It has "Don't show this window" (the same as unticking it in the options; it closes right away and you can turn it back on in /cj options) and "All options". Before, the gear opened the full options panel and you had to find the right checkbox.
- **Make Money: rows stay in place when you change quantities.** In Today's batch, pressing - or + changed a row's profit and moved it to another position, so you had to chase it. Now the order stays the same until the next scan, and the - and + buttons line up on every row.
- **Make Money shows what you'll actually pay.** Under "You invest in materials", a line says how much of it is already in your bags and how much you still pay (hover it to see which materials). Each row's tooltip says the same. The profit still counts your materials at their AH value, because selling them is the alternative: counting them as free would make it look profitable to turn expensive materials into things worth less.
- **Shift-click an item in the addon to search for it on the AH.** As in your bags: with the Auction House open, shift-clicking a row's item searches for it; with the chat open, it links it.

## 0.55.0

- **Now in German and French.** All texts, plus recipe and item names taken from the game itself. Pick the language with the selector at the top; German and French clients start in their own language. It's a first translation: if something reads oddly, tell us on Discord.
- **What's New window.** After an update, a window shows once what changed in that version. It doesn't show on a fresh install.
- **Routes no longer rely on cooldown recipes.** Transmutes (except Elemental Fire) and Mooncloth have cooldowns, but a route could ask for 186 Transmute: Iron to Gold in a row. Now they count for one skill point at most, and only while orange.
- **Transmute: Iron to Gold is no longer learned at 155.** Opening a Mining trainer made the addon read "Smelt Gold" (Mining 155) as Transmute: Iron to Gold, because both make Gold Bars, so routes learned it at 155 instead of 225. Fixed, and the wrong data is cleaned up on its own.
- **Each window has its own option, and a gear button.** The AH sell window can now be turned off separately from the AH shopping list. The windows that open on their own (vendor, AH, profession) have a gear button that opens the options, and the first time each one shows, the chat says it can be turned off.
- **Confirm before listing several units.** Listing more than one unit at once (sell window, Inventory, route) now asks first how many and at what price, so a stray click doesn't list a whole stack. Tick "Don't ask again" to turn it off; it can be turned back on in the options.

## 0.36.0

- **Crafted tools in the right place, and required steps can't be removed.** A tool you craft (Runed Golden Rod) now shows up at the step that needs it instead of at the start of the route. When a crafted item can't be bought (it binds when picked up, or nobody sells it), its row says so and has no ✕.
- **Clearer essence conversions.** The row now says what to convert and how many times ("Convert 129 Greater Magic Essence into 387 Lesser Magic Essence · right-click the Greater Magic Essence in your bags (129 times)"), and to buy them first if you don't have them.
- **The current step counts skill points, not crafts.** The highlighted step said "2 of 12 crafts" even when those 2 points came from crafting something else (like Bolt of Woolen Cloth for a later step), as if you had made 2 Woolen Capes. It now says "2/10 points · ~10 more crafts": how far you are in the step, and about how many more of that recipe you need.

- **Professions in a steady order.** The home page and Patterns sort the cards the same way: your professions first, then the rest. Within each group they follow a fixed order: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring, then the secondary professions, Cooking and First Aid. Before, each page sorted differently and the order changed as you leveled.

- **Error log.** The addon now keeps a record of its own Lua errors and of actions the game blocks, grouping repeats with a count. They still show up as usual (error window, BugSack…). Type `/cj errors` to list them and `/cj errors clear` to empty the list. When reporting a bug, this list helps a lot.

- **A star over the NPC you're sent to.** Clicking a trainer, vendor or auctioneer row still sets the route, and if that NPC is nearby it's also targeted and marked with the star raid marker, so it's easy to spot in a crowd. Clicking again removes the star. It does nothing in combat or if the NPC isn't nearby. In a group, only the leader or an assistant can set markers.

- **A new route planner: it looks at the whole route, like a GPS.** The old planner picked the best recipe one skill point at a time, then corrected itself in several passes. That was slow and could settle for a route that isn't the best overall. The new one looks at the whole route, the way a sat-nav finds the shortest road. It proposes complete paths from your level to your target, each recipe paying its trainer (and any tool it needs) once. It walks each path with real Auction House prices, intermediate crafts and what's in your bags. Then it improves the best one: craft or buy each material, try other recipes for a step, move step boundaries. Across 49 test scenarios (every profession, several skill ranges, with and without bags) it found a cheaper route in 33 and the same one in 16, and never a worse one, in about the same time. It also avoids zigzag routes, like alternating Bolt of Woolen Cloth and Woolen Cape every point. It also remembers the route it gave you, so when your bags change (turning scraps into leather) it keeps that route unless another one is really better. Started routes don't change. `tools/route_qa.py` compares it with the old planner.
- **Sell to the vendor from the vendor window.** When you open a vendor, the Crafter's Journal window next to it now also lists what the Profit view says to sell to the vendor and you carry in your bags: how many and what you get. Click a row to sell it, or "Sell all". As with buying, it only sells when you click.
- **Real vendor prices.** Forever's vendors don't always pay what the game data says: Hillman's Shoulders sell for 6s 59c, not 11s 99c, and the route counted the higher price. When you sell something to a vendor, the addon now notes what you actually got and uses that from then on. Hillman's Shoulders are already corrected.
- **What you have in your bags is valued correctly.** Using 10 of your 34 Medium Hide used to count as if you were selling those 10, best price first. But you keep selling the rest yourself, so what you actually give up is less. This changes when "With what you have" suggests a recipe.
- **Cured Medium Hide and Medium Armor Kit cost what Forever's trainer charges.** They're 2s and 2s 50c, not the original Classic 6s 50c. Prices seen at a trainer now also update a started route: its recipes stay the same, only what it costs to learn them changes.
- **Every trainer recipe gets checked.** When you open a trainer, the addon now reads its whole list, even recipes its filters hide. `/cj trainer` shows how many trainer recipes of each profession you've seen and how many differ from the data. It also lists recipes a trainer teaches that the addon doesn't know yet (like Gray Leather Boots), so they can be added.
- **"Get recipe" rows for the Auction House take you there.** Clicking them sets a route to the nearest auctioneer; with the Auction House open, it searches for the recipe.
- **Routes are recalculated less often.** Crafting or buying used to throw away the routes of all eight professions. Now only the routes that use your bags (and aren't started) are redone. Learning a recipe only redoes that profession's route, and browsing a trainer without learning anything redoes nothing. The home page only calculates routes for the professions your character has. The others are calculated when you open them, and their card then shows that route. Nothing is calculated while the window is closed.

- **The route no longer jumps around while you use your materials.** With "Use what you have in your bags" on and no route started, the route recalculates when your bags change, and turning Ruined Leather Scraps into Light Leather could suddenly switch it to a route asking for hundreds of Light Leather. The route was chosen as if you bought Light Leather but then costed as if you crafted it, so having more materials could give a worse route. Both now use the same decision, and the craft-or-buy check also tries materials seen in other routes. Converting your scraps now keeps the same route. Tip: press "Start route" before you start crafting or buying, and nothing in the route changes at all.
- **"You have" only counts your bags.** The shopping list, the vendor window and "use what you have" no longer count your bank or Auction House purchases waiting in the mailbox. The mailbox count kept saying "87 in the mail" after you had already picked up the scraps and turned them into leather.
- **A double click on "Start route" no longer releases it.** The button turns into "Recalculate route" in the same spot, so a quick second click used to undo the start.
- **"Hidden tips" instead of "Recipes removed from the route"** when the only thing you hid is a "With what you have" note: the route itself didn't change.
- **Removed "Actual" spending tracking.** The second row under the summary ("Actual: …") and the "sold" counts in the Profit view are gone. Matching each purchase and sale to the right route was unreliable, especially with routes started in several professions at once. Started routes, the shopping list and selling or posting from the Profit view work as before.
- **What you could make with what you have.** With "Use what you have in your bags" on, the route now looks at recipes your materials could level you with for a good stretch. If making one with what you have beats selling your materials and following the route, the route uses it. If it doesn't, a quote under the steps explains why: "With what you have you could make Elixir of Minor Fortitude (50–75): you only spend 3s 25c at the trainer and vendor, but you use 50 Earthroot and 25 Peacebloom worth about 1g 63s on the AH. Selling yours and following the route is about 1g 24s better." With a route already started, it tells you to recalculate when one would be worth it. When using your materials comes out about the same as selling them (less than 15% of what they're worth apart), the route uses them: no selling 68 bolts on the AH to save 3s. Not interested in a note? Its ✕ hides it for that recipe without changing the route; "Hidden tips" brings it back. It looks at every recipe your materials allow, not just the one giving the most points, so a Medium Armor Kit with your Medium Leather isn't missed because Cured Medium Hide alone wasn't worth it. What your materials would fetch is judged like the Profit view does: a realistic Auction House price only when the market looks good (a few units when it's unclear), the rest at the vendor, and never more units on the AH than are listed there now.
- **Trainer prices for Forever's new recipes.** Sulfuric Acid and Cerulean Dye now cost 1s 35c and are learned at Alchemy 40 (they showed "?" and 50). The skill 20 camp recipes (Mana Well, Camp Tent, Reagent Bot, Enchanted Lute, Sharpening Wheel, Faction Banner) are free: they're taught by the quest "Camping 101". Minor Mana Potion costs 90c at Forever's trainer, not 1s.
- **Real trainer prices, learned in game.** When you open a profession trainer, the addon now reads what each recipe really costs and the skill it requires, and remembers it. The route, the learn rows and the Recipes view use those prices instead of the built-in data, so a recipe whose price wasn't known ("?") shows its real cost once you've seen it at a trainer. Type `/cj trainer` to list the prices that differ from the addon's data (please send them in so they get fixed for everyone).
- **No more nearly-grey recipes just because they sell well.** When a recipe makes money selling to the vendor, that profit no longer grows with the extra crafts a nearly-grey recipe needs. Before, the route could pick Lesser Magic Wand at 8% (13 crafts per point) over Greater Magic Wand at 95%, or Curiously Tasty Omelet at 10% (83 crafts for 4 points), just because more crafts meant more items to sell. Each extra craft per point now also weighs a little, so a profitable recipe is only kept nearly grey when it really saves money.
- **Materials are either crafted or bought, never both.** For a material you can craft (Light Leather from Ruined Leather Scraps, bars...), the route now crafts all of it or buys all of it, whichever is cheaper counting 5c per extra craft. Before, it could ask for scraps and Light Leather at the same time, and crafting the scraps lowered what the shopping list said you still had to buy.
- **"Craft N×" steps show up right when they're needed.** A material that's only used to craft another material (Light Leather for a Fine Leather Tunic) now goes right before that one, instead of at the start of the route. Click it to open its recipe and craft what's missing, like the leveling steps; with a route started, it's highlighted as the current step while you still need it.
- **Buy a material instead of crafting it.** The "Craft 30× Light Leather" step now has a ✕: the route recalculates buying it ready-made on the AH, without its materials. "Recipes removed from the route" lists it and brings it back.
- **The current step starts with learning the recipe.** With a route started, if you don't know the recipe of the step you're on yet, the highlighted step is "Train …" / "Buy recipe: …", not the crafting step. Once you learn it, the highlight moves to crafting.
- **Learning a recipe updates the route right away.** When you learn a recipe at the trainer (or from a pattern) with a route started, its "Train"/"Buy recipe" step turns grey with a ✓ and "Learned", like finished steps. Without a started route, the route is recalculated.
- **Fixed started routes showing up on other characters.** Characters with the same name on the same realm (possible in Forever) shared routes and profession settings, and the first character to log in after per-character data was added got everyone's old settings. Each character now keeps its own, identified by its unique character ID. Because the old shared data can't be split between characters, routes started before this update are cleared once: start them again.
