# Crafter's Journal

## 0.44.0

- **New window next to the Auction House: what to sell there.** When you open the AH, a window like the vendor one lists what the Inventory tab says to sell on the AH: how many, at what price, for how long and what you keep after the cut. Click a row to list all the row suggests, as in the Inventory tab, or Shift-click to list just one and test the price (one listing per click, as the AH requires). Right-click a row to never sell that item. The bottom of the window explains the three clicks, one per line. The window sits to the right of the small shopping list, or right next to the AH, and hides when the main window is open there.
- **Item names in the vendor window use their quality color** (green, blue…), as in your bags.
- **Crafter's Journal uses other addons' AH scans.** Some addons (such as ForeverForge) request a full scan as soon as you open the Auction House. The server only allows one every 15 minutes, so Crafter's Journal could never scan. Now, when another addon requests a full scan with the AH open, Crafter's Journal reads that same data without asking the server again, and the chat says where it came from. It doesn't open the Crafter's Journal window for those scans, only when you press Scan yourself. Without another addon, scanning is still manual: press Scan.
- **Profession tools are never offered for sale.** Inventory and the window next to the vendor offered the Blacksmith Hammer for 3 copper, and without it you can't do Blacksmithing. Tools such as the hammer, Arclight Spanner, enchanting rods, Mining Pick, Skinning Knife and fishing poles are now kept.
- **"Not for sale" list.** Right-click an item's row in Inventory or in the vendor window and the addon stops offering it for sale. The items go to a new "Not for sale" section in Inventory, and a right-click there offers them again. It works for tools too, if you do want to sell one. Both windows show a line about the right-click above their sell rows, so you don't have to discover it in the tooltip.
- **Every view asks for the same amount of each material.** The route's Materials view, the Shopping list and the vendor window each worked out how much you need in their own way, and the vendor window added the stage's safety margin to routes you hadn't started (14 Fine Thread instead of 12). They now share one rule: the current stage with its margin once the route is started and "by stage" is on, otherwise what's left of the route. Inventory still keeps everything your started routes need, not just the current stage.
- **The vendor never buys anything twice on its own.** If bought items didn't reach your bags within a couple of seconds, the addon assumed the purchase had failed and bought again, so a slow server could make it buy double (28 Fine Thread instead of 14). Now it waits for the items and, if some are still missing, tells you. The row keeps showing what's missing, so one click buys the rest.
- **Make money: every section can be collapsed.** Click a section header (+ / −) to fold it away. A folded section says how many rows it has, and the addon remembers which ones you folded.

## 0.43.0

- **Make money: sure sales to the vendor get their own section, with no limit of 20.** Each one says how many to craft: as many as still make a profit, until the AH runs out of materials or the next craft isn't worth it. Click a row to add its materials to the shopping list. The batch also allows more crafts, since its profit check was too strict. Thanks to Audren.
- **Make money: the minimum profit is a slider.** Drag it or use the mouse wheel instead of clicking through the values. It has more steps too: none, 10s, 25s, 50s, 1g, 2g 50s, 5g, 10g and 20g.
- **Make money: recipes you could learn.** A new section lists recipes you don't know yet for your professions that would make money now. Each row shows where to learn the recipe and what it costs, whether your skill is high enough, and the profit per craft. The tooltip says how many crafts pay back the cost of learning it. Recipes you can already learn come first.
- **Cooking no longer sends you to craft Basic Campfire up to 75.** The old Basic Campfire was still in the game data, but Forever only has the Basic Campfire Kit, which turns grey at 5, so those crafts gave no skill. Recipes that don't appear in the profession window are no longer used.
- **The window next to the vendor sells everything Inventory sends to the vendor.** Before, it only listed items your routes made. Now it also lists the rest of your bags that the Inventory tab says to sell to the vendor (junk, gear, recipes…), so you can sell with a click there without opening the main window. "Sell all" sells them all, including rows that don't fit, and says how many.
- **Uncertain items are listed for 8 h, not 24 h.** When the addon doesn't know whether an item sells, Inventory now weighs the AH against the vendor using the 8 h deposit (a third of the 24 h one) and lists it for 8 h. If it expires you lose less, and more items are worth trying on the AH. Items that sell well still go up for 24 h. The chat message says the duration used.
- **"The Auction House is busy" no longer repeats on every click.** It shows once, then tells you when the AH is ready again. If the AH stays "busy" for more than a few seconds, the next click tries to list anyway.

## 0.42.1

- **Crafter's Journal only scans when you press Scan.** When another addon (such as one that scans as soon as the Auction House opens) requested a full scan, Crafter's Journal used to read it along, so it looked like it had scanned on its own. Now it only notes the server's 15-minute wait so the countdown is right, and says that another addon used it.

## 0.42.0

- **Buying from the Shopping list keeps the quantity.** The Auction House could reset it to 1 right after Crafter's Journal set it; it's now set again for a couple of seconds after the results arrive.
- **Shopping lists ask for whole crafts.** Each material is split between the recipes that use it and rounded up to what each craft takes, in stage lists and whole-route lists alike: 20 Light Leather for two Light Leather Pants (10 each), not 15.

- **Opening the Auction House no longer closes Crafter's Journal.** If its window is open, it stays open next to the Auction House; the compact shopping list only shows when the window was closed (or when you close it).

- **The shopping list counts what your earlier steps made.** Materials a step crafted for later steps (the Light Leather of the Light Leather step, used by Embossed Leather Pants) used to be assumed in your bags; if you had sold or used them, you ran short halfway through the step. The list now asks for them and checks your bags, like any other material.
- **Ingredient steps only craft ahead what still gives points.** Extra crafts for later steps go in the ingredient's own step only while it would still give skill-ups when the later step starts; otherwise they're crafted when that step starts, as preparation, instead of all at once 100 levels earlier.

## 0.41.0

### Routes you can actually follow
- **Routes never ask for a step you can't make yet.** A step that starts with materials made in the previous step (the powder for Rough Dynamite) no longer runs out halfway with "craft 108 more at 40": whatever the step needs is ready before it starts. Materials already made by the route are always used before crafting more of the same.
- **Ingredient steps make everything later steps need, like the leveling guides do.** "Rough Blasting Powder 6-40 · 86 crafts · +22 for Rough Dynamite": the extra crafts happen in the powder step (the step gets longer with the points they give), so the dynamite step starts with all its powder in your bags. Clicking the step crafts the extra too.
- **No more back-and-forth steps.** A recipe used for a few points in the middle of another one (A, then B for 3 points, then A again) is dropped when it barely saves anything.
- **Specialization recipes only if you have the specialization.** Goblin, Gnomish, Armorsmith and Dragonscale recipes are only used by characters with that specialization; the Recipes tab says so.

### Trainer and vendor data from WoW Forever
- **Learn levels, trainer costs and who teaches each recipe now match Forever** (Bronze Warhammer at 100, Green Iron Leggings for 50s). "Train" rows take you to a trainer that really teaches the recipe: Green Iron Leggings is only taught by Expert trainers such as Bengus Deepforge.
- **Recipes Forever doesn't have are no longer used.** Trainer recipes no Forever trainer teaches (The Shatterer…) and patterns nobody sells in Forever (Spitfire, Ironvine, Season of Discovery leftovers…) show as such in the Recipes tab. Recipe vendors new in Forever are included.
- **What you see at a trainer counts.** Opening a trainer reads its whole list: a recipe its own rank's trainer doesn't have sends you to the next rank's trainer, and once you've seen a recipe at a trainer, its "Train" row takes you there.

### Started routes
- **"Recalculate route" is now "Leave route"**, and it needs a second click. A started route only changes when you decide.
- **"There's a better route from your level."** When you open a profession with a started route, Crafter's Journal checks once, in the background and only while the addon is open, whether a clearly better route exists from your level (at least 50s or 10% cheaper, or one that goes further). If so, a row above the route says what you'd save and which steps change: click it to switch, or ✕ to keep yours.
- **A route's tool row is marked done once you have the tool**, whether you bought it or crafted it yourself.

### Shopping
- **Green skill-ups are bought in batches.** When a step goes green, the stage shopping list asks for about 20 crafts' worth instead of the whole stage plus a margin, and the next batch once you use it up (with a chat notice). Heavy Dynamite from 135: 44 Heavy Blasting Powder instead of 118 up front.
- **The route's shopping list fills itself.** The top row of the Materials view says what the Shopping list has for a started route: the current stage or the whole route. Click it to switch; the Shopping list tab glows.
- **New Shopping list tab, right under Level professions.** One list for everything you want to buy, with progress, a ✕ per material and per section, and what's in your bags counted. With the Auction House open, clicking a material (or "Buy next") opens Blizzard's purchase with the quantity set; at the Auction House a compact panel shows the same list.
- **AH purchases only fill the cheap part** (up to 20% above the cheapest, or the plan's price); the chat says what the rest costs.
- **Buying from the AH no longer gets stuck on "Searching...".**
- **The vendor window always shows when you open a vendor**, and "Buy all" no longer buys several times what you need for items sold in lots.

### Other
- **The default target is the end of your current cap: 150, 225 or 300** (it was your level + 150, so 151, 152…). A target you type yourself is kept.
- **Progress while the route is calculated:** a percentage and a progress bar.
- **Make Money:** minimum profit button, the shopping list includes what you craft along the way, and "More options" no longer skips or repeats recipes.
- **Removed:** the options panel (clicking a route step always crafts), the Auction House icon (routes can always buy on the AH), money tracking, and the unfinished development features and debug commands. `/cj scan`, `/cj cancel`, `/cj minimap` and `/cj errors` stay.

## 0.40.0

- **Calculator: search in Auctionator and get the prices back.** If you have Auctionator installed, the calculator shows a "Search in Auctionator" button. It sends the selected calculation's materials (with the quantity you need) and the item it crafts to Auctionator's Shopping tab, using exact names as your game client shows them; materials bought from a vendor aren't included. With the Auction House open it searches right away, and the prices it finds go straight into the calculator (source "Auctionator") for materials and sale. Otherwise it creates the "Crafter's Journal" shopping list for when you open it. Whichever is newer wins: that search or your last full scan.
- **Calculator: an AH scan updates every saved calculation.** The calculator now refreshes when a scan finishes. A price you typed by hand stays until a newer scan brings that item; then the scan's price replaces it. Items the scan doesn't have keep your price.
- **Routes no longer get stuck on "Calculating the route…".** When the game loaded any item's data, a route that was still waiting for a vendor price started over from scratch, and so did the routes of every other profession. Long routes (Leatherworking 88 to 300) kept starting over and took up to 10 minutes to show. Now a route is only recalculated when an item it was actually waiting for arrives, never in the middle of a calculation (it recalculates once when it finishes), and only for that profession. The addon also asks the game for each item's data just once.

## 0.39.0

- **Routes now go up to 300 (Artisan).** The maximum target is 300 in every profession. If your saved target was 225 (the old maximum), it moves to 300 once; a route you've already started keeps its target.
- **New "Auction House" icon: routes without the AH.** Turn it off and the route only buys materials from vendors or uses what's in your bags, and everything you craft is sold to the vendor. Anything only available on the AH isn't used, so the route may cost more or stop earlier; when it stops, it tells you which materials no vendor sells.
- **New "Fewer crafts" icon.** Turned on, each craft counts as 1s when choosing the route, so it pays a bit more to avoid dozens of crafts of a green recipe. The total still shows what it really costs you. Off by default.
- **Vendor sell prices are no longer learned from a single sale.** One stray payment (a damaged item, money arriving at the same time) could save a wrong price: Greater Magic Wand showed 1s 68c while the vendor pays 2s. A price that differs from the known one now needs to be seen twice in a row, and unconfirmed prices saved by older versions are ignored.
- **The "(max ...)" label next to the target is gone.** The target still can't go above 300, and the filter icons are a bit smaller so all five fit in the header.

## 0.38.0

- **AH scans no longer fail because of other addons.** The server allows one full scan every 15 minutes per account, and other addons spend it too (ForeverForge_Auction scans on its own 3 seconds after you open the AH). Your scan then got nothing and gave up after 2 minutes. Now, when another addon asks for a full scan, Crafter's Journal reads that same data instead of waiting for nothing, and its countdown includes that scan.
- **The 15-minute scan limit counts for your whole account.** Scanning with one character used to leave the button enabled on your alts, where it couldn't work.
- **Scans that end with the same number of auctions are no longer dropped.** If the new scan had exactly as many auctions as the previous one and the game didn't send its event, the addon thought nothing had arrived.
- **Calculator: sell on the AH or through trade chat.** A new AH / Trade chat toggle in each calculation: through trade chat there's no AH cut and no deposit. Under the profit, both results side by side and the cut you save ("AH +19s 14c · Trade +27s 99c (8s 85c cut)").
- **Calculator: the price boxes are centered** so the numbers don't sit against the edge.
- **Calculator: the source no longer overlaps the total.** With a typed price, "typed" ran into totals with gold ("1g 48s 80c"). The total column now always has its own space.

## 0.37.2

- **The vendor window no longer buys more than you need.** After buying, it checked your bags after 1 second and, if the server hadn't delivered yet, bought again. Now it waits until the items stop arriving before asking for anything missing. It also no longer asks for the materials of intermediates you've already crafted (the salt for cured hides, for example). When a vendor sells in lots (5 at a time), the row says so.
- **The vendor window shows up more reliably.** It looks again when the game finishes loading the vendor's items, and it still uses the last profession you looked at after you open Make money.
- **Every route step shows its second-best recipe and why it's worse.** Under each step: "2nd: White Bandit Mask, +57s (sells back for less)". The tooltip compares the best alternatives with the route's recipe (crafts, cost and sale per craft, learning cost) and says when selling on the AH would make one better. If an alternative is cheaper, it says so: either the route pays a little more for fewer crafts, or it shows in green so you can switch with the ✕. It's worked out in the background without freezing the game.
- **Calculator: no more cut-off text.** The vendor discount moved to the note below ("vendor −10%" overlapped the total), and the notes under the sheet are shorter.
- **Inventory tells you that clicking a row sells it.** The line under the totals now says what a click does right now: sell it to the open vendor, list it on the open AH, or that you need to open one of them. No more looking for the item in your bags.

## 0.37.1

- **Inventory: no more AH suggestions that expire.** When the AH market for an item is uncertain (little data or mixed signals), the AH now has to pay more than the vendor even if the auction expires once and you lose the deposit (60% of the vendor price for 24 h, at least 1s per auction). Otherwise the item goes to the vendor, and the row and tooltip say the AH is risky. Items with a good market still go to the AH. (Crimson Silk Pantaloons: 30s on the AH vs 24s at the vendor, but 14s lost per expired auction.)

## 0.37.0

- **Full AH scans on Forever.** The server sends the auctions in batches and the addon kept only the first one (a scan stopped at exactly 1024 auctions), so many items had no price. It now waits until all batches have arrived and also reads any that arrive while it processes. It waits up to 2 minutes for the server (busy realms at launch took longer than the old 60 s).
- **Inventory tells you when disenchanting is worth more.** For green, blue and epic gear in your bags (soulbound too), when disenchanting it would clearly give more than selling it, the row says so ("disenchanted: ~1s 40c") and the tooltip lists the average dust, essences and shards at your AH prices (Classic disenchant table), and whether your Enchanting is high enough. Information only: the addon never disenchants anything.
- **Inventory no longer counts the AH deposit as lost.** You get the deposit back when the item sells, so what you keep on the AH is the price after the cut. More items now go to the AH instead of the vendor.
- **Inventory says when an item has no AH price.** Items missing from the last scan showed "vendor" as if the AH paid less; now the row says "no AH price".
- **Data updated to the launch client of WoW Forever (1.60.1.70862).** 94 vendor sell prices went down at launch (Bronze Dory 32s 53c → 4s 88c, Mithril weapons, the Chain sets, Volcanic and Nightscape gear...): profit and routes now use the real values. The addon also prefers the price the game reports for the item over its own data, so a future price change is picked up on its own.
- **New and changed recipes from the launch client:** new Enchant Boots - Speed (from a formula), First Aid Kit and Toxin Study; Disorienting Smog Potion removed; Shining Mithril Helm, the Heart of the Mountain formulas and Greater Mender's Potion can be learned earlier; Draught of Water Walking is learned from a recipe.
- **Merchant's Favor recipes for leather and mail shoulders work again.** The game renamed them ("Shoulder" → "Shoulders") and they no longer showed their Merchant's Favor price.
- **Recipe search no longer shows an empty list.** If you had scrolled down the list, searching (for example "bag") left the results out of view. The list now goes back to the top when you search.
- **New Inventory tab (bag icon).** What to sell from your bags, where and how much you get. Items sell on the Auction House when that leaves you more than the vendor, after the cut and deposit; the rest go to the vendor. Items that went up in price are listed at the top ("sell it now"). What your started routes still need is kept, not sold. With the vendor open, one button sells everything that goes to the vendor. Clicking a row lists it on the AH or sells it, depending on which is open.
- **New Calculator tab (note icon).** Work out how much a recipe makes you, like a spreadsheet. Search a recipe to add it: you see its raw materials (what you craft yourself, like a Bolt of Silk Cloth, is broken down into its cloth), quantity, price per unit and where it comes from (the cheapest of your last AH scan and the vendor, with your reputation discount in your best capital city), then the sale, the AH cut and the profit. The deposit is shown apart, with a 2 h / 8 h / 24 h selector (5% / 20% / 60% of the vendor price, at least 1s per auction as Forever charges now; one auction per stack): you get it back when the item sells, so it isn't taken from the profit. Every price has gold, silver and copper boxes you can type in; clearing them goes back to automatic. Save as many calculations as you want and delete them with ✕; prices you type stay in that calculation only.
- **New Make money tab (coin icon).** What to craft today with the recipes you know because it sells for more than it costs, using your last AH scan. Its shopping list also shows in the AH panel, next to your started routes.
- **The addon no longer reads or saves anything from your mailbox.** What earlier versions saved is deleted the first time you log in.

## 0.36.0

- **Crafted tools in the right place, and required steps can't be removed.** A tool you craft (Runed Golden Rod) now shows up at the step that needs it instead of at the start of the route. When a crafted item can't be bought (it binds when picked up, or nobody sells it), its row says so and has no ✕.
- **Clearer essence conversions.** The row now says what to convert and how many times ("Convert 129 Greater Magic Essence into 387 Lesser Magic Essence · right-click the Greater Magic Essence in your bags (129 times)"), and to buy them first if you don't have them.
- **The current step counts skill points, not crafts.** The highlighted step said "2 of 12 crafts" even when those 2 points came from crafting something else (like Bolt of Woolen Cloth for a later step), as if you had made 2 Woolen Capes. It now says "2/10 points · ~10 more crafts": how far you are in the step, and about how many more of that recipe you need.

- **Professions in a steady order.** The home page and Patterns sort the cards the same way: your professions first, then the rest. Within each group they follow a fixed order: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring, then the secondary professions, Cooking and First Aid. Before, each page sorted differently and the order changed as you leveled.

- **Error log.** The addon now keeps a record of its own Lua errors and of actions the game blocks, grouping repeats with a count. They still show up as usual (error window, BugSack…). Type `/cj errors` to list them and `/cj errors clear` to empty the list. When reporting a bug, this list helps a lot.

- **A star over the NPC you're sent to.** Clicking a trainer, vendor or auctioneer row still sets the route, and if that NPC is nearby it's also targeted and marked with the star raid marker, so it's easy to spot in a crowd. Clicking again removes the star. It does nothing in combat or if the NPC isn't nearby. In a group, only the leader or an assistant can set markers.

- **A new route planner: it looks at the whole route, like a GPS.** The old planner picked the best recipe one skill point at a time, then corrected itself in several passes. That was slow and could settle for a route that isn't the best overall. The new one looks at the whole route, the way a sat-nav finds the shortest road. It proposes complete paths from your level to your target, each recipe paying its trainer (and any tool it needs) once. It walks each path with real Auction House prices, intermediate crafts and what's in your bags. Then it improves the best one: craft or buy each material, try other recipes for a step, move step boundaries. Across 49 test scenarios (every profession, several skill ranges, with and without bags) it found a cheaper route in 33 and the same one in 16, and never a worse one, in about the same time. It also avoids zigzag routes, like alternating Bolt of Woolen Cloth and Woolen Cape every point. It also remembers the route it gave you, so when your bags change (turning scraps into leather) it keeps that route unless another one is really better. Started routes don't change. `tools/route_qa.py` compares it with the old planner.
- **Sell to the vendor from the vendor window.** When you open a vendor, the Crafter's Journal window next to it now also lists what the Profit view says to sell to the vendor and you carry in your bags: how many and what you get. Click a row to sell it, or "Sell all". As with buying, it only sells when you click.
- **Real vendor prices.** Forever's vendors don't always pay what the game data says: Hillman's Shoulders sell for 6s 59c, not 11s 99c, and the route counted the higher price. When you sell something to a vendor, the addon now notes what you actually got and uses that from then on. Hillman's Shoulders are already corrected.
- **What you have in your bags is valued correctly.** Using 10 of your 34 Medium Hide used to count as if you were selling those 10, best price first. But you keep selling the rest yourself, so what you actually give up is less. This changes when "With what you have" suggests a recipe.
- **Cured Medium Hide and Medium Armor Kit cost what Forever's trainer charges.** They're 2s and 2s 50c, not the original Classic 6s 50c. Prices seen at a trainer now also update a started route: its recipes stay the same, only what it costs to learn them changes.
- **Every trainer recipe gets checked.** When you open a trainer, the addon now reads its whole list, even recipes its filters hide. `/cj trainer` shows how many trainer recipes of each profession you've seen and how many differ from the data. It also lists recipes a trainer teaches that the addon doesn't know yet (like Gray Leather Boots), so they can be added.
- **"Get recipe" rows for the Auction House take you there.** Clicking them sets a route to the nearest auctioneer; with the Auction House open, it searches for the recipe.
- **Routes are recalculated less often.** Crafting or buying used to throw away the routes of all eight professions. Now only the routes that use your bags (and aren't started) are redone. Learning a recipe only redoes that profession's route, and browsing a trainer without learning anything redoes nothing. The home page only calculates routes for the professions your character has. The others are calculated when you open them, and their card then shows that route. Nothing is calculated while the window is closed.

- **The route no longer jumps around while you use your materials.** With "Use what you have in your bags" on and no route started, the route recalculates when your bags change, and turning Ruined Leather Scraps into Light Leather could suddenly switch it to a route asking for hundreds of Light Leather. The route was chosen as if you bought Light Leather but then costed as if you crafted it, so having more materials could give a worse route. Both now use the same decision, and the craft-or-buy check also tries materials seen in other routes. Converting your scraps now keeps the same route. Tip: press "Start route" before you start crafting or buying, and nothing in the route changes at all.
- **"You have" only counts your bags.** The shopping list, the vendor window and "use what you have" no longer count your bank or Auction House purchases waiting in the mailbox. The mailbox count kept saying "87 in the mail" after you had already picked up the scraps and turned them into leather.
- **A double click on "Start route" no longer releases it.** The button turns into "Recalculate route" in the same spot, so a quick second click used to undo the start.
- **"Hidden tips" instead of "Recipes removed from the route"** when the only thing you hid is a "With what you have" note: the route itself didn't change.
- **Removed "Actual" spending tracking.** The second row under the summary ("Actual: …") and the "sold" counts in the Profit view are gone. Matching each purchase and sale to the right route was unreliable, especially with routes started in several professions at once. Started routes, the shopping list and selling or posting from the Profit view work as before.
- **What you could make with what you have.** With "Use what you have in your bags" on, the route now looks at recipes your materials could level you with for a good stretch. If making one with what you have beats selling your materials and following the route, the route uses it. If it doesn't, a quote under the steps explains why: "With what you have you could make Elixir of Minor Fortitude (50–75): you only spend 3s 25c at the trainer and vendor, but you use 50 Earthroot and 25 Peacebloom worth about 1g 63s on the AH. Selling yours and following the route is about 1g 24s better." With a route already started, it tells you to recalculate when one would be worth it. When using your materials comes out about the same as selling them (less than 15% of what they're worth apart), the route uses them: no selling 68 bolts on the AH to save 3s. Not interested in a note? Its ✕ hides it for that recipe without changing the route; "Hidden tips" brings it back. It looks at every recipe your materials allow, not just the one giving the most points, so a Medium Armor Kit with your Medium Leather isn't missed because Cured Medium Hide alone wasn't worth it. What your materials would fetch is judged like the Profit view does: a realistic Auction House price only when the market looks good (a few units when it's unclear), the rest at the vendor, and never more units on the AH than are listed there now.
- **Trainer prices for Forever's new recipes.** Sulfuric Acid and Cerulean Dye now cost 1s 35c and are learned at Alchemy 40 (they showed "?" and 50). The skill 20 camp recipes (Mana Well, Camp Tent, Reagent Bot, Enchanted Lute, Sharpening Wheel, Faction Banner) are free: they're taught by the quest "Camping 101". Minor Mana Potion costs 90c at Forever's trainer, not 1s.
- **Real trainer prices, learned in game.** When you open a profession trainer, the addon now reads what each recipe really costs and the skill it requires, and remembers it. The route, the learn rows and the Recipes view use those prices instead of the built-in data, so a recipe whose price wasn't known ("?") shows its real cost once you've seen it at a trainer. Type `/cj trainer` to list the prices that differ from the addon's data (please send them in so they get fixed for everyone).
- **No more nearly-grey recipes just because they sell well.** When a recipe makes money selling to the vendor, that profit no longer grows with the extra crafts a nearly-grey recipe needs. Before, the route could pick Lesser Magic Wand at 8% (13 crafts per point) over Greater Magic Wand at 95%, or Curiously Tasty Omelet at 10% (83 crafts for 4 points), just because more crafts meant more items to sell. Each extra craft per point now also weighs a little, so a profitable recipe is only kept nearly grey when it really saves money.
- **Materials are either crafted or bought, never both.** For a material you can craft (Light Leather from Ruined Leather Scraps, bars...), the route now crafts all of it or buys all of it, whichever is cheaper counting 5c per extra craft. Before, it could ask for scraps and Light Leather at the same time, and crafting the scraps lowered what the shopping list said you still had to buy.
- **"Craft N×" steps show up right when they're needed.** A material that's only used to craft another material (Light Leather for a Fine Leather Tunic) now goes right before that one, instead of at the start of the route. Click it to open its recipe and craft what's missing, like the leveling steps; with a route started, it's highlighted as the current step while you still need it.
- **Buy a material instead of crafting it.** The "Craft 30× Light Leather" step now has a ✕: the route recalculates buying it ready-made on the AH, without its materials. "Recipes removed from the route" lists it and brings it back.
- **The current step starts with learning the recipe.** With a route started, if you don't know the recipe of the step you're on yet, the highlighted step is "Train …" / "Buy recipe: …", not the crafting step. Once you learn it, the highlight moves to crafting.
- **Learning a recipe updates the route right away.** When you learn a recipe at the trainer (or from a pattern) with a route started, its "Train"/"Buy recipe" step turns grey with a ✓ and "Learned", like finished steps. Without a started route, the route is recalculated.
- **Fixed started routes showing up on other characters.** Characters with the same name on the same realm (possible in Forever) shared routes and profession settings, and the first character to log in after per-character data was added got everyone's old settings. Each character now keeps its own, identified by its unique character ID. Because the old shared data can't be split between characters, routes started before this update are cleared once: start them again.
- **Scroll indicator in the recipe list.** A thin bar on the right of the recipe collection shows where you are in the list and how much is left; drag it to move. It only shows when the list doesn't fit.
- **Profit view shows how many you carry.** Each crafted item now says how many are in your bags ("3 in bags"), so you can see what you can sell or post right now.
- **The shopping list next to the Auction House only shows started routes.** The arrows switch between professions where you pressed "Start route" (and are hidden when there's only one). With no route started, it tells you how to start one.
- **Sharper icon on "Recipes removed from the route".** It's now drawn at its real size instead of stretched and pixelated.

## 0.35.0

- **Start route.** New "Start route" button under the summary: it locks the route you've chosen. While you buy on the Auction House, from vendors or take items from your mailbox, neither the route nor its cost change, and what you buy for it no longer shows up as free materials. Steps you've already passed are marked as done. Prices are frozen too: a new Auction House scan doesn't change the route's totals (not even after a reload) until you choose — "Update prices" keeps the same route with the new prices, "Recalculate route" looks for a new route. Playing never unlocks it (leveling up, buying, crafting), and when you reach your target it stays as "Route completed!" until you recalculate. Changing it on purpose does: "Recalculate route", the target, an option icon or removing a recipe.
- **Live progress while you craft.** With a route started, the step you're on is highlighted and shows how many crafts you've done and how many are left ("11 of 22 crafts · 11 left"); finished steps turn grey with a ✓ as soon as you reach the end of them, and you get a "Route completed!" message when you hit your target.
- **Planned and actual.** With a route started, the summary at the top shows a second row with what you've actually spent and earned since you started it: vendor and Auction House purchases, trainer costs and deposits, and what you get for selling what you craft (to vendors, and Auction House sales read from your mail). Only route items count.
- **Shopping list at the vendor.** Opening a vendor that sells something on your shopping list shows a window right next to it: what you're still missing, with a "Buy all" button (or click a row to buy just that one), and what you already have, greyed out. Purchases go one at a time and are checked when they arrive.
- **Auction House purchases waiting in the mail.** Auction House purchases arrive by mail in Forever: they're now counted as spent when you buy them, and the shopping list counts them as yours while they wait in your mailbox ("2 in the mail"), so it doesn't ask you to buy them again.
- **Sell and post from the Profit view.** With a vendor open, click a crafted item to sell what the route says to sell; with the Auction House open, click it to post it at the suggested price. Otherwise the click marks the nearest vendor or auctioneer on your map. Nothing is ever bought or sold without your click. The Profit view also shows how many of each item you've sold since starting the route.
- **Craft from the route.** Click a crafting step and it opens your profession with that recipe selected and starts crafting what's left of that step (at most what your materials allow). Prefer to only open the recipe? Turn off "Craft when clicking a route step" in Options → AddOns → Crafter's Journal.
- **Remove a recipe from the route.** Every step of the route now has a small ✕: click it if you don't want to use that recipe (for example, because its pattern is sold far away) and the route is recalculated with the next best option. A row at the end of the route brings removed recipes back.
- **No more expensive patterns for a handful of crafts.** The route could tell you to buy a pricey pattern (e.g. Deviate Scale Cloak for 70s on the Auction House) and then use it for only 3 crafts. Recipes you have to buy now only make it into the route when they pay off for the points they're actually used for.
- **"Use bags and bank" now picks a better route.** If the materials you're carrying make a different route cheaper overall (for example, leather in your bags makes Embossed Leather Gloves cheaper than buying Pattern: Deviate Scale Cloak), the route switches to it. Routes are compared as a whole, so it won't zigzag between recipes just to use up your materials.
- **Clickable shopping list.** Click an Auction House material to mark the nearest auctioneer on your map, or, with the Auction House open, to open its purchase with the quantity you're missing already filled in (just press Buy Now). The panel next to the Auction House does the same. Click a vendor material to mark the nearest vendor that sells it, or, with that vendor open, to buy what you're missing.
- **Live shopping list.** Each item now shows how many you have and how many are still missing for the rest of the route ("You have 12/20 · 8 missing"), and it updates as you buy, take items from your mailbox or craft. With a route started, items move between "buy" and "you already have it" based on what you carry right now.
- **Each character has their own routes.** Started routes and each profession's settings (target, options, removed recipes) are saved per character: your other characters don't see them, and you get them back when you log in with that character again.
- **Picks up where you left off.** Closing and reopening the window during a session takes you back to the page you were on, including the view you had open in a profession.
- **Refreshed look.** Bigger Route / Shopping list / Profit buttons with a large icon, like the Dungeon Journal's, more breathing room around everything, the Dungeon Journal's browns on the profession pages, both home pages and the recipe collection, and a color of its own for each profession card.
- **Easier to read at a glance.** The route now has a level column on the left: the level range of each crafting step ("72–75"), and the level where you learn a recipe, train a rank or get a tool. The shopping list and the Profit view use the same column for quantities, so all three views look alike.
- **Cleaner routes.** Where the route used to alternate two recipes every single point (e.g. Light Leather and Light Armor Kit from 1 to 20), it now sticks to one when that costs next to nothing extra: Leatherworking goes from 32 steps to 14.
- **Merchant's Favor recipes, your choice.** Recipes bought with Merchant's Favor tokens and camp reputation used to be counted as free in the route. Now they're left out by default, and a new Merchant's Favor icon next to the bag and quest icons lets you include them (they cost tokens, not gold; you need the camp reputation each one asks for).
- **Routes in progress on the home page.** Professions with a started route get a green border and a "Route in progress" note on their card.
- **Fixed recipe skill requirements.** 362 recipes learned from patterns, plans and formulas asked for less skill than they really need in Forever (e.g. Pattern: Dark Leather Tunic was shown at 50 but needs Leatherworking 75), so the route could tell you to learn a recipe you couldn't learn yet. They now use the exact skill each pattern requires.
