# Crafter's Journal

**Level every profession the cheapest way and collect every recipe — built for WoW Forever.**

## 📈 Level your professions for less

- A **step-by-step route** from your current skill to 225 for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Cooking and First Aid.
- Uses **real Auction House prices** from your own scans, and what you already carry in your bags.
- Tells you **what to craft, how many, what to buy** and where to learn each recipe.
- **Trainers by rank:** every step sends you to a trainer who really teaches that rank in Forever, with a **waypoint on your map** (TomTom and Shortest Path supported).

## 📖 Your recipe collection

- Every recipe of each profession in a **journal-style list**, like the Dungeon Journal.
- See at a glance which ones **you know**, which you **can learn now** and which you're **missing**.
- For each recipe: **where to get it** (trainer, vendor, quest, drop with creature levels and zones, reputation, Merchant's Favor), what it **costs to craft**, what it **sells for** and your **profit**.
- Filters, search and a progress bar to complete your collection.

## 🌍 English and Español

Switch language at any time: texts, recipe names and item names.

## How to use

1. Open it with **/cj** or the minimap button.
2. Open the Auction House and press **Scan** to get real prices (the server allows one full scan every 15 minutes).
3. Pick a profession and follow the route, or open the collection to hunt recipes.

## Feedback

This is a beta for WoW Forever. Found a bug or a wrong trainer/recipe source? Leave a comment or open an issue on GitHub.
